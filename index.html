    Specialized Early Childhood Development Qualifications

    ECD Level 4 Certificate

    NQF Level 4 | 120 Credits

    This qualification provides a foundational understanding of early childhood development for practitioners working with children from birth to school-going age.

    Modules Include:

    • Child Development (0-6 years)
    • Health and Safety in ECD
    • Educational Play and Activities
    • Communication with Children and Parents
    • Basic Classroom Management

    Duration: 1 Year Full-time

    Career Pathways: Assistant Teacher, Home-Based ECD Practitioner, Daycare Assistant

    Course Details

    ECD Level 5 Diploma

    NQF Level 5 | 240 Credits

    Advanced qualification for those seeking leadership roles in early childhood development settings or wanting to establish their own ECD centers.

    Modules Include:

    • Advanced Child Development
    • ECD Center Management
    • Curriculum Development
    • Financial Management for ECD
    • Legal Requirements and Compliance

    Duration: 18 Months Full-time

    Career Pathways: ECD Center Manager, Curriculum Developer, ECD Trainer, ECD Entrepreneur

    Frequently Asked Questions

    For ECD Level 4: Grade 12 certificate or equivalent. For ECD Level 5: ECD Level 4 certificate or relevant experience in the field. All applicants must pass an interview and show passion for working with young children.

    Additional requirements include a clear criminal record and medical fitness certificate as you'll be working with children.

    ECD Level 4 graduates can work as assistant teachers, home-based ECD practitioners, or daycare assistants. ECD Level 5 graduates can pursue careers as ECD center managers, curriculum developers, educational trainers, or start and manage their own registered ECD centers.

    Many graduates also work as ECD consultants, assessors, or moderators for ECD training providers.

    Yes! Our programs are registered with the South African Qualifications Authority (SAQA) and accredited by the Quality Council for Trades and Occupations (QCTO). Graduates receive nationally recognized certificates that allow them to register with the Department of Social Development to operate ECD facilities or seek employment in registered centers.
